Main Menu

In this game, you will take control of a dragon and engage in a turn-based battle against one of four unique enemies. Both you and your enemy will begin with a maximum of 100 health points. Whichever of you loses all of your health points first loses the game. You have access to four unique abilities: Fireball, Rod of Asclepius, Lightning Storm, and Black Hole. Rod of Asclepius is a unique ability that restores some of your health points. Balance your healing and attacks well, and you will be victorious!
